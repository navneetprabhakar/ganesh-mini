# Gate 3/4 training-entry assessment plan — 2026-09-17

Status: active local assessment; no training or GCloud action

## Purpose

Convert the terminal Gate 2 qualification into an exact, auditable training
input lineage and decide whether scaled corpus construction may begin. This
assessment does not reinterpret the V2 or V9 qualification results, open an
evaluation surface, execute training, or authorize GCloud usage.

## Qualified seed selection

Freeze the complete qualified inventory and one deterministic anchor for each
of the 120 capability/language/task-type cells:

- generation inventory: retain all 114 admitted V2 records spanning the 60
  generation cells, and mark the lexicographically first record ID in each
  cell as its deterministic anchor; and
- repair: use all 60 records in the exact order of the sealed V9 complete
  candidate corpus, bound to the sealed V5 assignment rows that V9 inherited;
  each is the sole repair-cell anchor.

Every selected row must bind its record hash, source identity, rights, lane,
task type, primary capability, assigned cross-cutting dimension, and upstream
qualification evidence. Selection must be reproducible without reading an
evaluation prompt, reference, output, or verifier result outside the already
sealed content-free separation boundary.

## Entry checks

The assessment passes only when:

1. upstream qualification seals and file hashes match the frozen V2 and V9
   artifacts;
2. the lineage contains exactly 174 unique record IDs, exactly 174 unique
   source identities, and at least one row in every
   capability/language/task-type cell;
3. every row grants prompt, answer, test, redistribution, and model-training
   rights;
4. all 174 source identities are unique and source hashes, revisions, and
   licenses are present;
5. the deterministic anchor matrix is exactly 10 records in every one of the
   twelve lane/task-type pairs;
6. the anchor matrix has exactly 12 records per primary capability and 24 per
   scheduled cross-cutting dimension; and
7. the V9 complete-cohort separation seal and V2 qualification seal remain
   bound unchanged.

## Decision boundary

A passing assessment authorizes local construction planning and execution for
the intended 900-row pilot corpus. All 174 qualification records count toward
the six-per-cell capability target. Construction supplies the exact per-cell
deficit: 246 additional generation records and 300 additional repair records,
546 total, producing 720 capability records. Broad replay then supplies 180
records, fifteen per lane/task-type pair. New qualification-derived drafting
remains capped so that total new-record drafting across qualification and
scale does not exceed the controlling 1,200-draft ceiling.

Construction must retain the V9 canonical test-author/executable contract,
unique source stimuli, global separation, sealed lineage, at most one
candidate correction, the 2% normalized-scaffold-cluster ceiling, and two blind
manual reviews per primary capability. Prior failed optimizer-only,
checkpoint-only, capacity-only, lane-scheduling-only, and PCGrad mechanisms
remain terminal and may not be presented as a new hypothesis.

The 900-row corpus still requires a separate sealed integrity, balance,
quality, separation, token-budget, and runtime package before training. Any
GCloud inspection, upload, creation, execution, teardown, or billing remains a
hard approval boundary.

## Hard stops

Stop if any upstream seal differs, any cell or deterministic anchor is missing,
any cell has multiple anchors, any source is reused, rights or lineage are
incomplete, evaluation data enters the lineage, the 900-row construction
exceeds its draft ceiling, or the final training package fails its integrity
or separation checks. Do not replace a failed entry check with an unsealed
heuristic or silently select another record.
